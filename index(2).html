How we got here

The original labels had no signal to learn

GEPA scored each prompt on 3 examples. The spread between prompts is smaller than the noise 3 examples produce, so a surrogate could not learn anything.

Noise per score, by evaluation size: 0.25 at 3 examples, 0.04 at 150, 0.03 at 300.

The multi-round loop tied the seed on generate and failed on ensure

Generate is a tie within noise (±0.03). Since the 7B features the loop used don't predict score, the tie likely comes from the generator copying good prompts, not from BO. A random-target run would test this.

Next: check the baseline, then get prompts from outside GEPA

  • Grouped cross-validation: hold out whole GEPA runs, so a prompt's parent and siblings are never in training. If the 0.47 holds, raw-embedding distance carries over to new runs; if it drops, it was partly recognizing near-copies.
  • A one-shot prompt pool (about 100 ensure prompts from one meta-prompt, no reflection), scored the same way. It shows where GEPA's best falls among simply sampled prompts, and gives a dataset free of GEPA lineage for the distance question.
  • 72B ReElicit on the generate stage, to close out ReElicit on both stages.
  • Later, to discuss: BOReFT learns its own low-dimensional space over hidden states, a different answer to what distance between prompts should mean. Compared at equal evaluation budget, not prompt against prompt.
